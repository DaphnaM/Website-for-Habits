Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a to-do?

Type into the input at the top of the list and press Return. To add a subtask under the item you just created, press Tab instead.

How do I set a due date or a reminder?

Open a to-do and set its due date. To be notified, allow notifications for Done when iOS asks — if you dismissed that prompt, you can re-enable it in iOS Settings → Notifications → Done.

What does snoozing do?

Snoozing hides a to-do from your lists until the time you pick, then brings it back. Snoozed items stay visible in the Snoozed list in the meantime, so nothing gets lost.

How does the home-screen widget work?

Long-press your home screen, tap +, search for Done, and add the small or medium widget. It shows the top items from your first list and lets you check them off without opening the app. The widget needs iOS 17 or later.

Can I add a to-do from another app?

Yes. Share text or a link from any app, choose Done in the share sheet, and it will be added as a new to-do. You can also ask Siri to add one.

What exactly does a share link expose?

A share link publishes a read-only view of just that to-do or list at a long, unguessable web address. Anyone holding the link can read it without signing in, so only send it to people you intend to share with. It never allows edits, and never exposes your account or anything else in it. Delete the link in the app to revoke access immediately.

What is AI Connect?

It lets an AI assistant such as Claude read and update your to-dos for you. In Settings → AI Connect you create an API token and paste it into your assistant. Anyone with that token has full access to your to-dos, so treat it like a password — and revoke it in the app when you stop using it.

Do my to-dos sync across devices?

Yes. As long as you're signed in with the same account, changes appear on your other devices automatically.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your to-dos, attachments, share links, and API tokens are permanently deleted right away. This cannot be undone. If you'd rather we did it for you, email daphna.mergi@gmail.com from your account's email address.
