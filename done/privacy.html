Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

Introduction

Done is a to-do app built and operated by Daphna Mergi ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Done collects, why it collects it, and the control you have over it.

The short version: Done stores the to-dos you write so they can sync to your devices. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or shared with data brokers.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address: used to create your account, sign you in, and recover access
  • Password: stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider — we never see or store your actual password
  • User ID: a unique identifier generated when your account is created

Your To-Do Content

Everything you enter in the app is stored so it can sync across your devices:

  • To-do titles, notes, and subtasks
  • Due dates, reminder times, and snooze times
  • List and project names, and the order you arrange them in
  • Completion state and the time an item was completed
  • Archived items, until you delete them
  • App preferences, such as list order and highlight settings

Attachments

If you attach a file, photo, or video to a to-do, that file is uploaded to private storage and associated with your account. Done requests camera and microphone access only at the moment you choose to record a photo or video for an attachment; it does not access your camera, microphone, or photo library at any other time.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, we store the push token your device issues (an Apple Push Notification service device token, or a web push subscription in the browser) so a reminder can be delivered at the time you set. Push tokens identify a device installation, not you personally, and are deleted with your account.

Diagnostics

Done does not include any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or attribution SDK. We do not build usage profiles and we do not track you across apps or websites. Our hosting provider keeps standard, short-lived server logs (including IP address) for security and abuse prevention, as any web service does.

Features That Share Your Data — and How to Control Them

Two optional features can expose your to-dos beyond your own account. Both are off unless you turn them on, and both can be switched off again at any time.

Share Links

When you create a share link for a to-do or a list, Done generates a long, randomly-generated, unguessable token and publishes a read-only page at that address. Anyone who has the link can view that to-do or list without signing in, so treat a share link as public. Share links are read-only — they never permit edits — and they expose only the item you shared, never your account, your email, or the rest of your data. Deleting the share link in the app revokes access immediately.

AI Connect

Done can connect to an AI assistant (such as Claude) through an MCP endpoint. If you choose to create an API token in Settings → AI Connect, any assistant you give that token to can read and modify your to-dos on your behalf. Your to-do content is then sent to whichever AI provider you connected, and that provider's own privacy policy and data-retention terms govern what happens to it there — we have no visibility into or control over that. Treat the token like a password, share it only with tools you trust, and revoke it in the app when you are done. Revoking a token cuts off access immediately.

On-Device Data

So that the home-screen widget and Siri shortcuts can work without opening the app, Done mirrors your sign-in session into the iOS Keychain and a small set of display preferences into a shared App Group container. This data stays on your device, is protected by iOS, and is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to operate the app — specifically to:

  • Create your account and sign you in
  • Store your to-dos and sync them across your devices
  • Send the reminders you schedule
  • Serve the share links and AI connections you explicitly create
  • Keep the service secure and prevent abuse

We do not use your content to train machine-learning models.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored with Supabase, a managed database and authentication provider. Protections include:

  • Encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for everything the app sends or receives
  • Encryption at rest on the database and file storage
  • Row-level security policies enforced by the database, so one account's queries cannot return another account's rows
  • A private attachments bucket, scoped per user and reachable only through short-lived signed URLs

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we do not collect data we do not need, which is the most reliable protection available.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your data leaves our systems only in these cases:

  • At your direction: when you create a share link, or connect an AI assistant via an API token
  • Service providers: Supabase, for database, authentication, and file storage (Supabase Privacy Policy); Apple, to deliver push notifications (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Legal requirements: if we are compelled by valid legal process, or need to protect our legal rights

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleting an individual to-do or attachment removes it. Deleting your account removes everything, as described below.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access every piece of data on your account, directly in the app
  • Correct any to-do, note, date, or preference at any time
  • Delete individual items, individual attachments, or your entire account
  • Withdraw sharing by revoking any share link or API token

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to request a portable copy of your data or to object to processing. Email us and we will honour those requests.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data in either of two ways:

  1. In the app (immediate): open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your to-dos, attachments, share links, API tokens, push registrations, and login are permanently removed right away.
  2. By email: write to the address below from your account's email address, and we will permanently delete your data within 30 days.

Account deletion is irreversible and we cannot restore deleted data.

Children's Privacy

Done is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

International Users

Done's infrastructure is operated by Supabase and may process and store data in countries other than your own, including the United States. By using Done you consent to that transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Done after a change means you accept the revised policy.
