Capture a task in a keystroke. Snooze it until it matters. Check it off from your home screen. No accounts to configure, no clutter to manage.

Built for Getting Things Out of Your Head

Capture Instantly

Type and hit Return. Press Tab to nest a subtask. Share text from any app, or just ask Siri.

Snooze Until It Matters

Push a task out of sight until this evening, tomorrow, or next week — and have it come back on its own.

Home-Screen Widget

See your top tasks at a glance and tick them off without ever opening the app.

Calendar View

See what's due when, and drag a task to a different day to reschedule it.

Shareable Lists

Publish any list or task as a read-only link. Revoke it the moment you're finished.

Works With Your AI

Connect an assistant like Claude and let it read and update your to-dos for you.

Quiet by Design

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your to-dos sync across your devices and stay yours.
